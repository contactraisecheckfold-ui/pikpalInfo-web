Privacy Policy for pokerNote

Updated September 28, 2026. pokerNote is developed by Michael Yang (raisecheckfold.com).

Your poker records

Player notes, hand ranges, tables, seats, locations, and sessions are stored in the app’s local database. No app account is required. Local record storage is separate from the advertising data described below.

Backups and exports

When you export a JSON backup or CSV file, you choose where it is saved. Cloud folders, shared copies, and device backups are handled by the services you use. Manage those copies separately. Restoring a backup replaces the app’s current records.

Advertising and tracking

The app includes Google AdMob advertising. Advertising may involve data collection and tracking. You can manage the app’s tracking permission in your device settings.

Managing your data

You can manage player and session records in the app. Removing the app removes its local data; exported files and backups must be managed separately. Keep a backup before restoring data or removing the app if you want to retain your records.

Contact Us

If you email support, your email address and the information you send are used to respond to your request. Avoid including private player notes unless needed to explain your issue.
