How do I add a new player note?

Open Players and tap +. Enter the player’s name and your note, then tap Add Player. You can also choose a color, mark the player as a favorite, or set a hand range.

How do I record a session?

Open Sessions and tap +. Enter the session details, including buy-in, add-on, cash-out, time, and location. Use the All, Month, or Year filter to review your records and summary.

How do I back up or export my data?

Open Function → Backup & Restore. Choose Export Backup to save a JSON file to Files, or Export CSV to select players, sessions, locations, tables, or seats for a spreadsheet.

What happens when I restore a backup?

In Function → Backup & Restore, choose Restore Backup and select a JSON backup. Review the contents before confirming. Restoring replaces all current data and cannot be undone. Export a backup first if you want to keep your current records. CSV files cannot be used to restore data.

Does this app sync my notes across devices?

Records are stored locally on your device; there is no automatic cloud sync. Export a JSON backup to keep a separate copy or restore it on another device. Restoring replaces the destination device’s current data.

Which languages does the app support?

The app is available in English, Spanish, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese. French and Japanese are website translations.
