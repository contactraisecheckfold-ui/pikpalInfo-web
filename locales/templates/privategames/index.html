One project. Two sides of the table.

Private Games connects players with hosted games. Player helps you discover and book; Provider helps hosts publish games and manage seats, waiting lists, and player records.

Support (English)

Browse before you join

Browse public listings as a guest. Search by game type or city, then sign in to see game details and reserve a seat.

Keep your place

Reserve an available seat or join the waiting list. Check My Bookings for status changes and automatic promotion when a seat opens. Cancel when your plans change.

Private Games Provider

Host games. Manage your table.

Support (English)

Publish the details that matter

Create cash games or tournaments with a public area, start time, capacity, and house rules. Keep the exact venue address separate from the public listing.

Run the table from one place

Track reservations and waiting players, seat arrivals, and record departures. Manage your player roster and private notes alongside your games.

Publishing new games requires an active provider membership. See support for subscription and billing details.

Help for both apps

Find help with bookings, provider memberships, account deletion, and contacting support. The existing support and privacy pages are shared by Player and Provider.
