Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026. DogKona is developed by raisecheckfold.com. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app.

Your recordings stay under your control

DogKona records camera video and microphone audio only when you start recording. Recordings, imported photo/video backgrounds, preferences, and your selected saving-folder bookmark are stored on your device. The developer does not receive these files or access your camera or microphone remotely.

No tracking or analytics

DogKona does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or a developer-operated server. The app does not collect personal data for the developer or sell personal data.

Files, sharing, and backups

If you choose a destination folder or use Share, your selected files are copied to that destination or shared through the service you choose. A cloud storage provider may upload those copies according to its own privacy policy. iOS-managed device backups may include app data. Apple and your chosen providers handle their services independently.

Permissions and local storage

Camera access is used for video capture. Microphone access is used for video with sound and audio-only recording; silent video does not need it. System pickers provide access to the photo, video, or folder you select. Preferences, file metadata, and available storage are accessed to save settings, organize files, and check space before recording or cleaning up old loop clips. You can change camera and microphone access in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Your recordings remain until you delete them, uninstall the app, or enable loop recording that removes eligible older local video clips within your chosen storage budget. Exported and shared copies are separate; manage or delete them in their destination. Choose a different background or the nature scene to replace an imported background.

Support and contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact raisecheckfold.com at contactraisecheckfold@gmail.com. If you email support, your email address and information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please do not send private recordings unless needed for an issue you want help with. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to respond and resolve the request.

Updates

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. The current version is available in the app and on the public privacy-policy page.
