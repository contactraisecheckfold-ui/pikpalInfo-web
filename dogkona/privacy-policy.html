Privacy Policy for DogKona

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

raisecheckfold.com operates the DogKona mobile application (the "Service" or "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related support services.

By using DogKona, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us as well as certain data automatically when you interact with the App:

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Details: If you register or log in, we collect your email address, display name, and authentication identifiers. If you use third-party sign-in providers (such as Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In), we receive authentication tokens and the basic profile information you authorize them to share.
  • Pet Profile Information: You may input information about your dog(s), including dog name, breed, date of birth/age, weight, gender, spay/neuter status, microchip number, and veterinarian notes.
  • Care & Activity Logs: Data you create regarding your pet's daily routines, including walk duration and distance, feeding times, potty tracking, medication logs, and vaccination milestones.
  • Photos & Media: Photos you choose to upload for your dog's profile or journal entries. We only access your device's camera or photo library with your explicit permission.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: Any information you provide when contacting our support team via email.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Information: If you choose to enable location permissions to track dog walks or calculate route distances, we collect GPS coordinates while the walk tracking feature is active. We do not track your location in the background when walk tracking is inactive, nor do we sell your location data.
  • Device & Usage Data: We may collect standard technical data including your device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash logs to troubleshoot technical issues and maintain app stability.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you enable notifications for reminders (e.g., medication, walk, or feeding alerts), we collect a notification token (such as Firebase Cloud Messaging token) to deliver those alerts to your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, lawful purposes, including:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the core features of DogKona.
  • To store and organize your dog's health, care, and activity history.
  • To send you scheduled reminders and notifications that you set up.
  • To diagnose bugs, optimize performance, and enhance user experience.
  • To respond to your questions, feedback, and customer support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or abusive activity.

3. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers solely to perform essential app functions under strict confidentiality and security terms:

  • Google Firebase: We may utilize Google Firebase services for backend database hosting (Firestore), cloud file storage, authentication, and push notifications (FCM). Data is hosted securely on Google Cloud servers. Review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Apple Sign-In & Google Sign-In: If you use these identity providers to access your account, their respective privacy policies apply to authentication.
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting: We may use privacy-preserving tools to understand app performance and resolve crashes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide you with the Service and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

How to Delete Your Data: You have full control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated pet records directly within the app at any time by navigating to:

Profile > Settings > Delete Account

Upon account deletion, all personal data, dog profiles, logs, and photos are permanently and irreversibly removed from our servers. You may also request complete data deletion by emailing us at contactraisecheckfold@gmail.com.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access and Update: You can review, update, or edit your pet and profile data directly in the app at any time.
  • Device Permissions: You can enable, disable, or revoke camera, photo library, location, or push notification permissions at any time via your device's System Settings.
  • Data Portability & Deletion: You may request an export of your data or complete removal of your records.

6. Security of Your Data

The security of your data is paramount to us. We employ standard technical and administrative safeguards, including encrypted communications (HTTPS/TLS) and secure database access controls, to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Children's Privacy

DogKona is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal obligations. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.
