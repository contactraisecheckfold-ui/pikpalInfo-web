Frequently Asked Questions

Can I manage more than one dog in DogKona?

Yes! You can add and switch between multiple pet profiles seamlessly within the app, each with its own health history, reminders, and activity logs.

How are my pet's data and photos stored?

Your data is stored securely. If you use cloud sync or create an account, your information is securely backed up so you never lose your dog's memories or medical records. You also have full control to export or delete your data at any time.

How do walk tracking and location permissions work?

When you track a walk, DogKona requests location permission to accurately record your walk distance and route. Location data is only used during active walk tracking and is never sold or shared with advertisers.

How do I set up medication or appointment reminders?

Navigate to the Reminders tab, tap "Add Reminder", choose the type (Medication, Food, Vet, Grooming), specify the time and repeat frequency, and enable notifications on your device.

How can I delete my account and data?

You can permanently delete your account and all associated pet data at any time. Simply navigate to Profile > Settings > Delete Account within the app, or reach out to us at contactraisecheckfold@gmail.com with your account email.
