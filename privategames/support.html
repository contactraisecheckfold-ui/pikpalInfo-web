Private Games Support

Help for Private Games Player and Private Games Provider.

Contact support

Email contactraisecheckfold@gmail.com. Include the app name, device model, app version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private venue addresses.

Bookings and game access

Guests can browse public listings. Sign in to reserve a seat or join a waiting list. VIP games require that provider’s VIP membership. Contact the provider about the event itself. Sample games are demonstrations and cannot be joined.

Delete your account

In the updated iOS apps, open Settings → Delete account. Review the consequences, verify your identity and confirm. Deletion applies to your shared account in both apps. You are signed out immediately and automatic cleanup starts within a few minutes; retries can take longer. Arrange any settlement and export records first. Deleting an account does not refund or transfer funds. De-identified bookkeeping amounts may remain with other users, without your identity or private notes.

Provider subscriptions

Provider Monthly is an auto-renewing membership required to post new games. The U.S. price is $9.99 monthly, with a one-month introductory trial for eligible new subscribers. Local pricing and eligibility are confirmed by Apple. Creating a profile is free. Players do not need this subscription.

Use Restore purchases in Settings → Provider membership if needed. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple subscription settings. Deleting your Private Games account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Contact Apple about Apple billing or refund requests.

Report a concern

To report inappropriate game content, impersonation or unsafe behavior, email support with the game title or game link and a description of the concern. Do not include sensitive personal or financial information. Providers are responsible for complying with the requirements where their games take place.
