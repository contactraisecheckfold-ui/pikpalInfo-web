Private Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

raisecheckfold.com operates Private Games Provider and Private Games Player (together, “Private Games”). This policy explains how we handle information when you browse games, create an account, host a game, reserve a seat, join a waiting list, or subscribe as a provider.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication information. Firebase Authentication processes email and password sign-in. If you use Google or Apple sign-in, we receive the account information those services share, such as your name, email address, and sign-in identifier. Apple may provide a private relay email address.
  • Provider and game information: provider profile details and the information submitted with a game, including its title, game type, stakes or entry details, date, time, capacity, public area, private venue address, and description.
  • Bookings and waiting lists: the games associated with your account, reservation status, queue position, participation status, and related timestamps.
  • Provider subscription records: an account-linked purchase identifier, Apple product and transaction identifiers, subscription status, expiration dates, and signed transaction information used to verify access. Apple processes subscription payments; Private Games does not receive your payment-card number.
  • Service and support information: technical request and error logs generated when operating the service, and information you send when contacting support.

You can browse public game listings without creating an account. Browsing still involves requests to our hosting and backend providers. Venue locations are entered by providers. If you choose “Use my location” in the player app, your device or browser asks for location access. We use that position on your device to filter games by approximate distance; the app does not save or upload your position to our Firebase database. You can choose a city without granting location access.

For nearby search, we store approximate coordinates for the public game area. The provider app sends the public city or area text to the operating system’s geocoding service to locate it. Coordinates are rounded to approximately a kilometre of precision. The private venue address is not used for public distance search. Distance estimates refer to the public area and may differ from the distance to the venue.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, publish and display games, manage reservations and waiting lists, show seat availability, verify provider subscriptions, prevent misuse, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support requests.

What other people can see

Public game listings, including the public area and game details supplied for publication, can be viewed by guests. Do not put a private address or other confidential information in a public title or description.

The private venue address is available to the hosting provider and players with confirmed or seated reservations. Players on a waiting list do not receive it through that reservation. Providers can view the booking and participation information needed to manage their games. Removing access cannot remove information someone has already seen or copied.

Service providers and disclosures

We use Google Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions to authenticate accounts, store data, and operate server-side features. Information is processed on Google’s infrastructure. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Apple handles App Store subscriptions and sends transaction and subscription-status information used to verify provider access. Google and Apple also process information when you choose their sign-in services. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information or use the current Private Games apps to serve advertising. We may disclose information when necessary to comply with applicable law, protect users and the service, or investigate abuse.

Storage, retention, and security

Account, game, and booking records are stored in Firebase. The current database is hosted in the United States; service providers may process information in other locations. Access controls restrict private records to the accounts and server processes that need them. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

We retain information as needed to operate your account, manage games and bookings, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Subscription verification and transaction records may need to be retained after account deletion to prevent fraud and address payment disputes. Deletion requests are reviewed for any records that must be retained for these purposes.

Your choices and requests

You may browse without signing in and cancel reservations through the app. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and associated information, email contactraisecheckfold@gmail.com from your account email address. We may ask for information needed to verify the request. Do not send your password or payment-card details.

You can revoke location permission in your device or browser settings and continue using the city filter. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription in your Apple account’s subscription settings. Requesting account deletion does not itself cancel an Apple subscription. Subscription cancellation does not itself delete your Private Games account.

Children

Private Games is intended for adults. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are below the legal age applicable to your participation. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

Policy updates

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or your account email.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact contactraisecheckfold@gmail.com.
